Working with statistical methods, developing models, and seeing whether they really deliver what they promise is something I truly enjoy. This is why I am applying for the position \inputfill{src/part1}

For my Master’s thesis at the Chair of Statistics and Econometrics, entitled “Tracking Inflation in the G20 at High Frequency and in Real Time: A Machine Learning Approach”, I examined how inflation can be nowcast using a large number of predictors. For this purpose, I worked with machine learning approaches and developed an end-to-end forecasting framework using 20 years of weekly Google Trends data. I automated the data collection, processed and transformed the high-frequency data, and implemented a mixed-frequency forecasting framework, all in Python. I developed and compared machine learning and time series models such as LSTM neural networks, XGBoost, autoregressive models, and a Random Walk benchmark. The thesis showed me how important data and quantitative models are for assessing developments and supporting decision-making. At the same time, I was able to deepen my knowledge of data science, statistical modeling, and programming, while learning to approach complex analytical tasks independently and in a structured way.

I have already been able to apply much of this knowledge in practice as a Working Student in the Statistical Methods and Labor Studies departments at the Institute for Employment Research (IAB). There, I worked with statistical methods and large datasets: I prepared, validated, and analyzed extensive survey and administrative data in R and Stata, including linked longitudinal data on more than 44,000 respondents, and presented the results in tables and visualizations for researchers. I also supported causal analyses of refugee mobility policies using propensity score matching, difference-in-differences, and regression kink designs. During my earlier internship at Ziraat Bank, I gained practical insight into retail banking, analyzing customer and portfolio data with SQL and preparing Excel-based analyses for branch management. Through my studies and work experience, I therefore bring a solid foundation in data analysis, model development, and reporting.
